💡 How FindLeads Works

1
Type a business type & city
Enter something like "barbershops" and "Austin, TX". FindLeads understands 100+ business categories.
2
We search OpenStreetMap
Your query is sent to the Overpass API — a free, open read-only interface to OpenStreetMap's global database of businesses, landmarks, and places.
3
We check each business for a website
OpenStreetMap stores whether a business has a website as a tag. We check the website and contact:website fields on every result.
4
Leads are flagged automatically
Any business without a website tag gets a bright No Website — Lead! badge. These are your warm prospects.
5
Reach out & pitch your services
Click "Open in Maps" to verify the listing, find the phone number, and get directions. Save your best leads to a named list and export as CSV to your CRM.

FindLeads is 100% open source and runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no API keys, no servers, no costs — ever. Business data is provided by the OpenStreetMap community under the ODbL license.
